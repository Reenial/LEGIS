**Example — Crisis Guard:** The team initially relied on browser location access when submitting disaster reports. When users denied permission, they could not provide a location. The team added manual location selection and tested both paths. This showed why a permission-dependent feature needs an alternative user flow from the start.

**Analysis of the example:** The account names the difficulty, the response and a specific lesson. It does not claim that the entire application was tested because one flow was checked.
